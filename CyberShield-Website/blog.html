Five Simple Security Practices

01

Use Strong Authentication

Use strong passwords and additional authentication controls where appropriate.

02

Keep Systems Updated

Apply security updates and patches according to your organization's maintenance procedures.

03

Protect Important Data

Identify sensitive information and apply appropriate access and protection controls.

04

Train Your Team

Help employees recognize phishing, social engineering and other common security risks.

05

Have an Incident Plan

Prepare procedures for responding to and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.
